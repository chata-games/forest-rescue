# Meadow ground material

Painterly forest meadow ground with layered short grass, moss, clover, sparse violet flowers, and soft leaf litter in the Forest Rescue style.

- 1536x1024 full-bleed ground plate, no path
- Top-down view
- Emerald and moss-green palette with restrained violet and cyan accents
- Broad readable brushwork; no high-frequency noise, borders, grids, or seams
