# Player 2 — Mushroom Hollow (efficiency optimizer lens)

I play to min-max. I want to know what a unit costs, what it does, and whether the math works. In ~40 minutes I lost seven runs of Mushroom Hollow, six of them with defenders built and paid for, and I could never observe a single point of damage dealt by anything I built. The level's systems are either broken or completely silent about themselves. Both possibilities are report findings.

## Session log

The overlay does not cover the HUD, so the first screen of the game shows a stale, contradictory state. [img/player-2-start-stale-hud.png]

## Graphical observations

1. Canvas is 1720 px wide in a 940 px window after Replay. What I saw: first load renders the full level correctly; every Replay afterwards shifts the world right by ~410 px and fills the left 45% with a blank green slab. The spawn-side of the map, including rings ring-3, ring-11, ring-31, ring-30, and the on-path chokepoint ring, becomes invisible and unclickable. -> A player who replays loses over half the map with no way to see or defend it, and no visual explanation. -> Constrain the canvas to its container width and re-measure on resize; the fix should also restore the pause/mute buttons, which the same overflow pushes to x=1680/1442. (High impact, small effort.)
2. Defender bar cuts off three of six cards. What I saw: Firefly Beacon, Mushroom Shaman, Sprig Sentinel fully visible; Thornvine Bramble half visible; Wisp Willow and Dewdrop Nymph entirely off-screen. The cheapest unit is the one I couldn't read. -> Players on narrow windows cannot evaluate half the roster, and cannot tell the bar scrolls (if it does). -> Wrap or scroll the card bar, and never render a selected highlight on a card whose label is off-screen. (High impact, small effort.)
3. Stale HUD behind the start overlay. What I saw: "Wave 1 / 8" and 100 mana readable behind the title, while the real level says Wave 1 / 10. -> The very first screen contradicts the game's own counter and invites distrust. -> Hide or reset the HUD beneath menu overlays. (Medium impact, small effort.)
4. Campaign map numbering. What I saw: two interleaved chains using the same numerals (two 2s, two 4s, two 5s, two 6s) and at least one marker with no visible label where level 04 actually opens. -> I clicked blind coordinates and got lucky; a human would tap "4" expecting level four and get whatever that node is. -> Unique node ids or a single chain; hover/tap preview naming the level. (Medium impact, medium effort.)
5. Darkness modifier vs sprite readability. What I saw: poachers are small dark figures on a dark trail; in the shifted layout I initially mistook ring glows for defenders and vice versa. Placed sentinels are 2-3 px of green distinguishable from ring glow. -> The level's one visual identity gimmick (darkness) is spent on hiding player assets, not just enemy ones. -> Give placed defenders a bright outline or icon; darken the trail less than the surroundings. (Medium impact, medium effort.)
6. Game Over screen. What I saw: a clean modal, readable copy ("The Heartwood was breached."), one obvious Replay button, and the idle mana total (593) frozen behind it. -> The modal itself works; it just has nothing useful on it. -> Show wave reached, enemies leaked, mana wasted. Optimizers read end screens. (Low impact, small effort.)

## Play-technical observations

1. Defenders have no observable combat effect. What happened: across runs 3-6 I placed up to four paid defenders (mana deductions confirmed on the HUD, sprites visible on rings). Wave 1 always ended with exactly 1 heart left, the same outcome as run 1 with zero defenders. In run 6 I watched three poachers pass within a sprite width of a sentinel: no projectile, no animation, no HP change, no slow. [img/player-2-poachers-pass-defender.png] -> This is the game. Everything else is decoration on top of a tower-defense core that does not appear to resolve attacks, or whose attacks are imperceptible. -> If targeting is broken, fix it; if units attack but the feedback is invisible, add projectiles, hit flashes, enemy HP bars, and floating damage. Either way this is the ship-stopper. (High impact, large effort if logic, small if feedback.)
2. No build phase; wave 1 starts on level entry. What happened: the "Wave 1" badge is up the moment the level renders, and enemies spawn within seconds. Any time spent reading six cards or looking at a dark map costs hearts; every first minute of planning was punished with a 4-heart leak. -> New players lose before they understand the interface; my run 1 was lost entirely to reading. -> Delay the first wave behind an explicit "Start" (or a 15-20s countdown), or spawn enemies only after the first placement. (High impact, small effort.)
3. Information starvation for decision-making. What happened: cards show a name and a cost, nothing else. No range circle during placement, no stats on hover or tap, no tooltip on placed units, no enemy HP bars. I could not answer the basic optimizer questions: what does a Sprig Sentinel do, what is its range, is a 60-mana Beacon better than 1.2 sentinels, what is a poacher's HP? Thornvine's placement failure came with no message, so I cannot even tell if it is ring-restricted, path-only, or broken. -> Costs without outputs are not an economy, they are a slot machine. I defaulted to spamming the cheapest visible unit, which is exactly the behavior a strategy game should not induce. -> Show range circles on placement and on tap, one line of stats per card (damage / rate / range or effect), and reject invalid placements with a reason. (High impact, medium effort.)
4. Mana economy generates waste, not decisions. What happened: passive income measured at ~5.1 mana/s (422 -> 526 over 20.3s) with no visible cap; mana idled at 443-593 during defeats while the only purchasable things were units that do nothing and rings I could not reach. Flowers, the +25 bonus the start screen advertises, ignored four direct taps. -> Income the player cannot convert is pure noise, and flowers that don't respond train players to stop clicking the world. -> Cap or bank mana visibly, make flower hitboxes generous with a pickup burst, and make sure there is always something worth buying. (Medium impact, small effort.)
5. Waves overlap with no clear signal. What happened: the wave counter advanced to "Wave 2 / 10" while wave 1 enemies were still walking; per the level file the gap between waves is delayAfter=3s after the last spawn, not after the last kill. -> Combined with instant wave 1, the level is a ~40-second total wipe for any opening that is not frame-perfect; there is no pacing to read and no moment where the player is ever caught up. -> Gate the next wave on field clear (plus a short breather), or at minimum show "Wave 2 incoming" with a timer. (High impact, small effort.)
6. Replay is a core loop and it is broken twice over. What happened: Replay works as a button, but it re-enters the level with the broken canvas layout and instantly-running wave 1. Seven runs in a row, the retry loop itself punished me. -> Fix the resize, add the build phase, and the retry loop becomes the tutorial it should be. (High impact, inherited from 1-2.)

## Top 5 suggestions

1. Make defenders actually fight, or show that they do. Category: play-technical. Evidence: seven runs, up to four placed units per run, zero observed kills; wave 1 outcome identical with and without defenders; run 6 poachers passing an adjacent sentinel untouched. Proposal: fix target acquisition against ring positions, and add projectiles plus enemy HP bars so combat is verifiable by eye. Impact: high. Effort: M-L (logic) / S (feedback only).
2. Fix the canvas resize after Replay. Category: graphical. Evidence: canvas rect 1720x601 in a 940 px viewport on every run after the first; left 45% blank slab; 5 of 9 rings and the pause/mute buttons unreachable; reproduced seven times. Proposal: size the canvas from its container on every level entry and on window resize. Impact: high. Effort: S.
3. Add a build phase before wave 1. Category: play-technical. Evidence: wave 1 badge visible at level load in every run; four-heart leak in every first minute, including a run lost entirely while reading the card bar. Proposal: hold wave 1 until the player places the first defender or presses Start; 15s countdown minimum. Impact: high. Effort: S.
4. Surface unit stats and ranges. Category: play-technical. Evidence: cards carry only name and cost; no tooltip, no range circle, no info on tap; Thornvine placement rejected with no message. Proposal: range circle on placement, stat line per card, tap-to-inspect on placed units, rejection reasons. Impact: high. Effort: M.
5. Fix the card bar and wave pacing together. Category: play-technical. Evidence: three of six cards off-screen at 940 px, including the cheapest unit; waves 1 and 2 overlap 3s after last spawn. Proposal: scrollable/wrapping card bar; gate waves on field clear. Impact: medium-high. Effort: S.

## Verdict

Not fun today, and not really playable: the run I can actually recommend is reading the card bar, which loses. The art, the trail, and the flavor text are genuinely charming, and zero JS errors in forty minutes says the engineering discipline exists. But the core loop currently punishes every rational action: planning, building, replaying, even clicking flowers. Fix defender combat resolution and the canvas resize first; everything else is polish on a game that needs its heart beating.
